# Known Limitations — SwasthyaScan AI

> This document lists known limitations of the SwasthyaScan AI prototype.
> It must be updated before any clinical or public deployment.

---

## 1. Not Clinically Validated

SwasthyaScan AI is a **research prototype** and has **not** been validated in
clinical settings. Its performance on populations different from the training
datasets is unknown. It must not be used for clinical decision-making.

## 2. AI Model Limitations

- The EfficientNet-B0 model was trained on publicly available CXR datasets
  (Shenzhen, Montgomery, TB Chest X-ray). Performance may vary on images from
  different scanners, populations, or clinical contexts.
- The model score is **not a calibrated probability**.
- Grad-CAM++ heatmaps are model explanation aids only — not clinical findings.
- `MODEL_VERIFIED=false` — no external clinical validation has been performed.

## 3. Image Quality Sensitivity

- Poor image quality (low contrast, rotation, incorrect laterality) may cause
  "Unable to Assess" results or unreliable scores.
- Only PA (posteroanterior) chest X-rays are expected. Lateral or AP views
  may degrade accuracy.

## 4. Binary Classification Only

The model predicts TB vs. Normal. It does not detect other pulmonary conditions
(pneumonia, cancer, pleural effusion, etc.).

## 5. Dataset Limitations

- Training data is sourced from specific geographic regions. Performance on
  populations from other regions is unknown.
- No paediatric-specific validation has been performed.

## 6. SwasthyaScan AI Chatbot Limitations

- The chatbot provides **general health education only**. It cannot and does
  not diagnose any condition.
- The chatbot works only with text the user types in the chat box. It does not
  have access to X-ray images, questionnaire answers, or model results.
- Responses are generated by the Groq API (using the `llama-3.1-8b-instant`
  model). Like all LLMs, it may occasionally produce inaccurate information.
  Always verify important health information with a qualified healthcare provider.
- The chatbot is restricted to TB and health-related topics. Off-topic requests
  are politely declined.
- Chat messages are sent to Groq's servers to generate replies. No messages are
  stored by SwasthyaScan AI. Users should not share personal health details,
  names, or identifying information in the chat.
- The chatbot does not have memory between sessions. Each new session starts fresh.

## 7. Environment Feature (Doctor-Patient Sessions)

- Environments expire after 24 hours. Data is deleted on expiry.
- The 8-character environment code is not a security mechanism — it is for
  convenience only. Do not use this system for real clinical data.

## 8. No Emergency Capability

SwasthyaScan AI is not designed for emergency situations. If a user reports
emergency symptoms (severe breathing difficulty, coughing blood, chest pain,
fainting), they must be directed to seek immediate medical care — and they are.
